Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Super League Bot ("the Bot") is a Discord application developed for use with Super League. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot accesses, how that information is used, and how it is stored.

1. Information We Process

To provide its functionality, Super League Bot may process information made available through Discord, including:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Discord server (guild) IDs
  • Discord role IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord interaction and command information
  • Team, manager, and staff assignments associated with Discord users
  • Server-specific Bot configuration
  • Usage information required for certain Bot features

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, precise location information, or other unrelated sensitive personal information.

2. Command Logging

Super League Bot includes functionality for logging certain staff commands. When enabled, command logs may contain the Discord user who executed the command, the user's Discord ID, the command used, the channel where it was used, and information supplied as command options.

These logs are sent to the Discord channel configured by server administrators. Access is controlled by the Discord server's permissions.

The Bot does not continuously monitor or store ordinary Discord conversations.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the Bot is used to manage Super League teams and rosters, managers and staff, player and team-related actions, permissions, roster and demand limits, server configuration, administrative command logging, Bot-generated notifications, contract offers, and operation and troubleshooting.

We do not sell personal information or use information processed by the Bot for targeted advertising.

4. Data Storage

Super League Bot stores operational information required for its functionality. This may include Discord IDs and information associated with teams, rosters, managers, staff, server configuration, permissions, and feature usage.

Data may be retained for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the operation and administration of Super League Bot.

5. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord's API and processes information made available to it through Discord's permissions and Gateway Intents. Discord separately processes information according to its own policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

Super League Bot does not sell, rent, or trade user information. Information processed by the Bot may be accessible to authorized Super League administrators or Discord server staff when necessary for operation and administration.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information stored by Super League Bot where deletion is technically and operationally possible.

Requests should identify the relevant Discord account or server and describe the information that should be deleted.

Some information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate administrative purposes.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information stored by Super League Bot. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Third-Party Services

The Bot relies on Discord and its API to operate. It may also rely on hosting or infrastructure providers necessary for operation. Information is not intentionally provided to advertising networks or data brokers.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be made available through the Bot's official information or documentation.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Super League Bot developer through the official Super League support or administrative channels, or contact simplyuncool_ on Discord.
