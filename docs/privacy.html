Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Super League ("Super League", "we", "us", or "our") operates an online league and its associated website, Discord community, competitions, teams, player and staff systems, and software services, including Super League Bot (collectively, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed through those Services, why it is used, and how it is handled.

1. Information We Process

Depending on which Services you use or interact with, we may process information made available through Discord, Roblox, league administration systems, or forms and interactions you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Discord user IDs, usernames, display names, server IDs, role IDs, and channel IDs
  • Discord interactions, commands, moderation actions, and relevant administrative activity
  • Team, player, manager, staff, roster, registration, and league participation information
  • League applications, submissions, requests, reports, disputes, and administrative records
  • Competition, match, fixture, result, statistic, disciplinary, transfer, contract, and transaction-related records where applicable
  • Server-specific Bot configuration, permissions, and feature settings
  • Information necessary for invite tracking, verification, security, anti-abuse, and league administration
  • Information you voluntarily provide through tickets, support requests, forms, or communications with league staff

When a user voluntarily uses Roblox account verification, the service may process information returned by Roblox through its OAuth authorization flow, including Roblox user ID, username/display name, and other basic account information permitted by the requested OAuth scopes. The resulting verification record may associate the Roblox account with the user's Discord account.

We do not intentionally collect Discord or Roblox passwords, Roblox session cookies, payment-card information, precise location information, or unrelated sensitive personal information through the Services.

2. League Information

Participation in Super League may require maintaining records about teams, players, managers, staff, registrations, rosters, transfers, contracts, matches, results, standings, statistics, disciplinary decisions, applications, and other league activities. Some of this information may be visible to other participants or the public when it is part of normal league administration, competition records, announcements, or published standings.

League records may be retained for historical, administrative, competitive-integrity, dispute-resolution, security, and record-keeping purposes. Removing an account or leaving the league does not necessarily require deletion of historical competition records where retaining them is reasonably necessary to preserve the integrity or history of the league.

3. Roblox OAuth Verification

The Roblox verification feature uses Roblox's OAuth authorization flow. When a user chooses to verify, the user is redirected to Roblox to authorize the requested access. The Bot does not receive or request the user's Roblox password or Roblox session cookie.

OAuth authorization data and access credentials are used only as necessary to complete and maintain the configured verification functionality. Verification information is used to establish a relationship between a consenting user's Roblox account and Discord account and, where configured, assign a verified Discord role.

4. Command, Moderation, Security, and Audit Logging

Super League Bot may log certain staff commands, moderation actions, configuration changes, security events, anti-abuse events, invite information, and other administrative activity when required for operation, accountability, or security.

Depending on the feature, logs may include the Discord user who performed an action, the affected server, channel, user, team, role, or record, the action performed, relevant command options, and timestamps. Administrative logs may be accessible to authorized Super League staff.

The Bot does not continuously monitor or intentionally store ordinary Discord conversations merely because they occur in a server where the Bot is present.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and administer Super League; manage teams, players, managers, staff, rosters, transfers, contracts, and league records; operate competitions and publish relevant standings, results, and statistics; process applications and support requests; perform Roblox verification; send notifications; enforce league rules; investigate disputes and misconduct; prevent abuse, fraud, raids, nukes, unauthorized changes, and other security incidents; maintain audit records; troubleshoot the Services; and maintain the security and integrity of the league.

We do not sell personal information or use information processed by the Services for targeted advertising.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Super League stores operational and league information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, its role in league history or administration, security requirements, legal obligations, and whether it is necessary to resolve a dispute or investigate abuse.

Historical league records, including competition results and related records, may be retained after a participant leaves Super League when reasonably necessary for accurate league history, competitive integrity, or administrative purposes.

7. Data Sharing

Super League does not sell, rent, or trade user information. Information may be accessible to authorized Super League administrators, moderators, managers, or staff when reasonably necessary to operate the league, administer teams and competitions, provide support, investigate incidents, or maintain security.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the Services, users, league integrity, or rights of others.

8. Discord, Roblox, and Other Third-Party Services

The Services rely on third-party platforms and infrastructure, including Discord and, where applicable, Roblox and Roblox OAuth. Those providers separately process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Super League does not control information collected directly by third-party platforms.

The Services may also rely on hosting, database, authentication, analytics, or other infrastructure providers necessary for operation. Such providers are used only as reasonably necessary to provide or secure the Services.

9. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal information stored by Super League where deletion is technically and operationally possible. Requests should identify the relevant Discord account or other record and describe the information requested for deletion.

Some information may not be deleted when it is reasonably necessary for league history, competitive integrity, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or other legitimate administrative purposes. Deleting Super League records does not affect information independently retained by Discord, Roblox, or other third parties.

10. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the Services. These measures may include access controls, permissions, logging, security monitoring, and anti-abuse systems. No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's and Minors' Use

Users must comply with the age requirements and terms of the platforms through which they access Super League. Super League does not knowingly request unnecessary sensitive information from minors. If you believe information has been collected improperly, contact us through the official Super League support or administrative channels.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Super League's Services, league operations, security systems, or data practices change. The latest version will be made available through the official Super League website.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Super League developer through the official Super League support or administrative channels, or contact simplyuncool_ on Discord.
